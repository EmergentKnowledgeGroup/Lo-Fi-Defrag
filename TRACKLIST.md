# Soundtrack Status

The Lo-fi Defragger soundtrack is still being curated. No music files are committed to this repository or bundled with the app today.

## Use your own music

Open `Playlist` in the app, then choose `Import` for selected files or `Folder` to scan a directory. Your tracks stay on your machine; the app stores playlist paths and metadata only.

Supported file extensions are `.aac`, `.aif`, `.aiff`, `.flac`, `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.mp4`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.wav`, and `.webm`. Playback ultimately depends on the codec support in Electron's Chromium runtime.

## Future curated release

When the original soundtrack is ready, publish it with clear credits and licensing, either as a separate release download or in a deliberately sized asset package. Do not add unfinished tracks, personal libraries, or unlicensed audio to the repository.

## Contributing music

Before submitting music, provide the track title, artist/credit, license or written permission, source format, duration, and whether redistribution is allowed. Keep large audio assets out of ordinary source pull requests unless the maintainers explicitly request them.
